About TCG Pro

Our Mission

To make trading card games welcoming for everyone by offering authentic sealed product at fair prices, and a place where new players and lifelong collectors can share the hobby.

Our Story

TCG Pro started from the idea that trading card games should be accessible to everyone. We all see the affect that scalpers have had on the TCG hobby, and we wanted to create a store that would be a safe place for players and collectors to get the products they want without having to worry about inflated prices or counterfeit items.

Today we stock Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and One Piece, and we keep the shelves focused on sealed, authentic product so you always know what you're getting.
